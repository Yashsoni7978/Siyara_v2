## 1. Keyword Strategy Framework

This master keyword map establishes a disciplined, search-intent-driven taxonomy for Siyara Innovations. Keywords are clustered into four distinct pillars:
1. **Core Local Service Keywords (Jaipur Focus):** High-intent commercial queries for agency services in Jaipur.
2. **Commercial & Small Business Problem Keywords:** Solution-seeking queries from business owners looking to build or scale their digital presence.
3. **Industry-Specific Vertical Keywords:** High-conversion long-tail queries matching Siyara's verified project experience in Healthcare, Real Estate, Interior Design, and Events.
4. **Informational & Topical Authority Keywords:** Educational content driving organic backlinks, top-of-funnel discovery, and E-E-A-T relevance.
