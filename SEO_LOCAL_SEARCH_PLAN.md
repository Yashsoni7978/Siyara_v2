## 4. Policy-Compliant Review Acquisition Process

Customer reviews on Google Maps are the #1 local ranking factor for competitive terms like *"web design company in Jaipur"*. However, Google strictly prohibits review gating, incentivized reviews (discounts for 5 stars), or fake testimonials.

### Siyara's Review Acquisition Workflow:
1. **Trigger Moment:** 7 days following a successful website launch or delivery of an SEO audit milestone.
2. **Channel:** Direct WhatsApp message from the studio team.
3. **Approved WhatsApp Review Request Template (Zero Incentives, High Authenticity):**
   ```text
   Hi [Client Name], we really enjoyed collaborating with you on the [Project Name] digital platform! 
   
   If you have 60 seconds, could you share your honest feedback on our Google profile? It helps other local businesses in Jaipur find our studio. 
   
   Here is the direct link: [Insert Short GBP Review Link]
   
   Thank you for trusting Siyara Innovations!
   ```
4. **Response Protocol:** The agency should reply publicly to every single review (both positive and constructive) within 48 hours, mentioning the specific service delivered (e.g. *"Thank you, Dr. [Name]! We loved designing the clinic website and local search setup for your practice."*), which naturally reinforces topical keywords in Google Maps.
